# Prompt for ChatGPT: Pitch Deck Image Generation (Slide by Slide)

**Role:** You are a seasoned professional presenter, product manager, and AI graphic designer at a top-tier tech company (like Meta). You know how to captivate an audience, weave technical details into a compelling narrative, and design stunning, minimalist presentation slides.

**Task:** I need you to create a complete 10-15 slide presentation deck for "WasteWise AI". 
For EACH slide, you must:
1. **Generate a 16:9 aspect ratio image using DALL-E 3.** The image must look like an actual presentation slide (sleek, modern, dark-mode aesthetic, corporate tech keynote style). 
2. **Include text in the image:** The generated image should contain the Slide Title and 2-3 minimal bullet points or a powerful metric. 
3. **Provide Speaker Notes:** Below each generated image, provide charismatic, persuasive speaker notes (what the presenter will actually say).

*Please generate these slides one by one or in batches if needed, but ensure every single slide is delivered as an actual generated image.*

**Project Context (WasteWise AI):**
WasteWise AI is a next-generation, AI-powered municipal waste management platform. It bridges the gap between citizens reporting street waste and the municipality dispatching trucks to clean it. We are replacing outdated, manual dispatch systems with a modern, automated intelligence layer.

**Key Features to Highlight in the Slides:**
1. **The Problem:** Outdated, manual, inefficient waste collection. Orphaned waste. High fuel costs.
2. **The Solution:** WasteWise AI - A unified, intelligent ecosystem.
3. **Citizen Portal:** Frictionless reporting with WebRTC Live-Camera enforcement (preventing fraudulent/old photos) and live GPS tagging.
4. **AI Computer Vision Engine:** Instantly analyzes citizen photos to estimate volume (m³), categorize the waste, and assign a priority/severity score (0-10) before a human even sees it.
5. **Officer/Admin Command Center:** A sleek MapLibre-powered triage center. Officers see clustered reports, view AI recommendations, seamlessly dispatch fleet vehicles, or manually override AI decisions.
6. **Smart Fleet Management:** Tracks trucks, drivers, and payloads. Built-in logic prevents loaded trucks from going into maintenance, ensuring waste is never orphaned.
7. **AI Dynamic Routing:** Uses TSP (Traveling Salesperson Problem) algorithms (via OSRM) to optimize truck routes on the fly, significantly saving fuel and overtime wages.
8. **Financial Analytics Dashboard:** Proving ROI to stakeholders by tracking fuel savings, operations costs, and cost-per-ton metrics.

**Design Guidelines for DALL-E:**
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 wide (Standard widescreen PPT format).
- **Aesthetic:** Dark mode, highly professional, glowing accent colors (emerald green, neon blue, or amber), glassmorphism UI elements, sleek sans-serif typography.
- **Content:** Keep the text inside the image short, punchy, and readable. Use abstract 3D shapes, maps, or UI dashboards as the background graphics.

**Tone of Speaker Notes:**
Visionary, authoritative, technically sound, and inspiring. We are solving a real-world infrastructure problem using cutting-edge AI, beautiful UI/UX, and robust architecture.
