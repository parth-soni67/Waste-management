# Prompt for Claude: Pitch Deck & Presentation Generation

**Role:** You are a seasoned professional presenter, product manager, and software developer at a top-tier tech company (like Meta). You know how to captivate an audience, weave technical details into a compelling narrative, and sell the vision of a product like a true keynote speaker. 

**Task:** Prepare the complete content for a 10-15 slide presentation deck for "WasteWise AI". For each slide, provide:
1. **Slide Title**
2. **Visual Suggestion:** (What should be on the screen? e.g., "A sleek dark-mode mockup of the Analytics dashboard showing ROI")
3. **Bullet Points:** (Minimal text for the slide)
4. **Speaker Notes:** (What the presenter will actually say. This must be charismatic, persuasive, and sound like a Meta Keynote).

**Project Context (WasteWise AI):**
WasteWise AI is a next-generation, AI-powered municipal waste management platform. It bridges the gap between citizens reporting street waste and the municipality dispatching trucks to clean it. We are replacing outdated, manual dispatch systems with a modern, automated intelligence layer.

**Key Features to Highlight:**
- **Citizen Portal:** Frictionless reporting with WebRTC Live-Camera enforcement (preventing fraudulent/old photos from device galleries) and live GPS tagging.
- **AI Computer Vision Engine:** Instantly analyzes citizen photos to estimate volume (m³), categorize the waste, and assign a priority/severity score (0-10) before a human even sees it.
- **Officer/Admin Command Center:** A sleek map-based triage center. Officers can see clustered reports, view AI recommendations, seamlessly dispatch fleet vehicles, or manually override AI decisions.
- **Smart Fleet Management:** Tracks trucks, drivers, and payloads. Built-in logic prevents loaded trucks from going into maintenance, ensuring waste is never orphaned.
- **AI Dynamic Routing:** Uses TSP (Traveling Salesperson Problem) algorithms to optimize truck routes on the fly, significantly saving fuel and overtime wages.
- **Financial Analytics Dashboard:** A module that proves ROI to stakeholders by tracking fuel savings, operations costs, and cost-per-ton metrics.

**Tone:**
Visionary, authoritative, technically sound, and inspiring. We are solving a real-world infrastructure problem using cutting-edge AI, beautiful UI/UX, and robust architecture.
